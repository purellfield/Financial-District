Our Mission

Strengthen Men Through Purpose-Driven Fitness

F3 Conejo is part of F3 Nation — a growing network of over 5,000 free, peer-led workouts for men across 400+ regions in the U.S.

We meet in public spaces across the Conejo Valley for early-morning, bootcamp-style workouts designed for men of all fitness levels. Our mission is to plant, grow, and serve small workout groups for men for the invigoration of male community leadership.

Getting in the best shape of your life is just a by-product. The workout draws men in — the relationships, brotherhood, and community keeps them coming back.

Leave no man behind, but leave no man where you found him.

Welcome, Friendly New Guy (FNG)

New to F3?

You've made it past your excuses. That's the first step to becoming the best version of yourself as a man. F3 does not require membership and is open to ALL men of ALL physical abilities.

The Truth

Men Need Brotherhood

There is one undeniable truth: men need authentic relationships with other men who are working to be strong fathers, husbands, and leaders. Isolation will destroy a man. We have the opportunity to change our community and the trajectory of our families. The workout is the magnet. The brotherhood keeps you coming back.

Tips for Your First Workout

What to Expect

01
Modify as Needed

If you haven't exercised in a while, slow down. Don't hurt yourself or get discouraged. Just show up, work hard, and give it your best. We are not competing against one another — we are proud of you for showing up at 5:30am.

02
Pick a Location

Visit our Locations page to find the AO (Area of Operation) that works best for you. Nine workouts across the Conejo Valley, Monday through Saturday.

03
Know the Lexicon

F3 has a language all its own. The PAX (participants), the Q (leader), the beatdown (workout), the COT (Circle of Trust). Check the Lexicon at f3nation.com so you're not lost in the gloom.

04
Just Introduce Yourself

If you're not meeting a friend at your first workout, simply show up and introduce yourself as an FNG (Friendly New Guy). You will be welcomed. All you need is an open mind, a positive attitude, and outdoor workout clothes.

We've Heard Them All

Top 5 Excuses Not to Show Up

01
"I need to get in shape before coming."

This is Excuse No. 1 on the F3 Hall of Fame chart. The only way to get in shape is to jump in with a group of men who will push you, challenge you, and refuse to let you quit. Stop waiting for ready.

02
"I already belong to a gym."

Gyms are comfortable, predictable, and a little lonely. Out in the gloom, men gather in freezing rain and blazing heat, pushing each other to grow — physically, mentally, emotionally. There's no treadmill setting for camaraderie.

03
"That's too early to get up."

By getting up before the world wakes up, you invest in yourself while everyone's still sleeping. You're done before the day begins — no guilt, no sacrificed family time. A win for you and your family.

04
"I'm already working with a personal trainer."

F3 offers more than a workout — it's an experience. Same accountability and push, but with a group of men working to improve themselves. And here's the kicker: it's 100% free.

05
"This sounds like a cult."

It's not a cult — it's a brotherhood. Men gathering for a common cause: to improve themselves, their communities, and to lead with purpose. It used to be called civic engagement. If that's cultish, we're proudly standing firm.

No More Excuses
